## Residual Required Fixes
- [R1] BLOCKING — May-side adequacy circularity (§4.3, §4.7): The definition of `semanticPullbackMay(E,S)` includes the disjunct `proj_i(x)=none` alongside `∃y, E(x,y) ∧ y∈S`. However, `E : Ω → β_i → Prop` is an abstract relation that is defined independently of `proj_i`. When `proj_i(x)=none` there is no witness `y : β_i` arising from `proj_i`, so whether `E(x,y)` holds for some `y` is unrelated to `proj_i`. The `none` disjunct in `semanticPullbackMay` must therefore be justified as a deliberate definitional choice — that `E` is treated as 'silent' (vacuously non-witnessing) when `proj_i(x)=none` — and this choice must be stated explicitly. Without this, the statement `preimageMay = semanticPullbackMay` (when both sound and complete hold) conflates two different sources of `none`-membership: (a) `proj_i(x)=none` in the preimage definition, and (b) the `none` disjunct in the semantic pullback. The manuscript must either (i) add a side-condition that `E(x,y)` implies `proj_i(x) = some y` (making the `none` disjuncts correspond), or (ii) explicitly state that `semanticPullbackMay` treats `proj_i(x)=none` as a convention for 'E has no witness at x' and justify that this convention is coherent with the Lean definition in Adequacy.lean.
- [R1] NEAR-BLOCKING — Misleading framing of `U*` restriction (§3.5): The manuscript states '`U* ∩ Dom_i ⊆ lifted(i)` は「弱化主張」ではなく、部分射影下で観測可能領域を明示した必要条件仮定である'. This framing is formally incorrect as stated: restricting from `U* ⊆ lifted(i)` to `U* ∩ Dom_i ⊆ lifted(i)` is logically weaker (fewer elements must satisfy the inclusion). The manuscript's intent appears to be that this is not a *weaker* scientific claim but a *more precise* one (avoiding an unprovable universal), but the word '弱化主張ではない' directly contradicts standard logical usage. Fix: rewrite to clarify that the restriction to `Dom_i` makes the hypothesis *logically weaker* (a subset is required to satisfy it) but *epistemically appropriate* given partial projections, and that the full unrestricted form `U* ⊆ lifted(i)` is a strictly stronger hypothesis available in the Lean 'ideal' variant.
- [R2] §6.2 mutation table: The column `detection_method` uses two heterogeneous labels — `uand_contradiction` (a U∧ judgement outcome) and `bound_shrinkage` (a numeric criterion on extracted values). The note beneath the table states 'expectation_satisfied=true does not always mean raw_judgement=contradictory', but the table header `detection_method` implies a unified detection mechanism. This asymmetry must be resolved: either rename the column to `detection_criterion_type` with values `{u∧_judgement, bound_metric}`, or add a column `detection_layer ∈ {U∧, U0/metric}` to make clear that the two mutations are assessed by fundamentally different predicates. As written, a reader tabulating 'mutation_detected_by_expectation = 6/6' may conflate the two detection mechanisms and infer a stronger uniform guarantee than the framework provides.
- [R2] §4.3 adequacy 運用帰結 block: The four-item list (false consistency, missed consistency, false contradiction, missed contradiction) is repeated almost verbatim in §4.7 under 'adequacy inclusionから得られる誤り方向'. The §4.7 copy lacks the §4.3 warning box ('以下は hSound/hComplete などの抽象仮定が成立する場合の形式的帰結') directly preceding the list. Readers who enter from §4.7 may read the error-direction claims as unconditional properties of the PoC pipeline. Add the same conditioning note at the head of the §4.7 reprise, or replace the reprise with a cross-reference to §4.3.
- [R2] §7.5 truncated sentence: The final sentence ends mid-word ('オフライン追試で SHA256 不一致が…'). This is an editorial incompleteness that must be resolved before publication; a truncated sentence in the reproducibility section creates direct doubt about whether the verification procedure is fully specified.
- [R3] BLOCKER — Script hash unverifiable at replay time: §7.2 documents external_validation.py SHA256 as `970c0fcc6d7407362a5b1a31fd0f9526a7cd05d8bbd0944032e054565dc8206b` (2026-02-17 run). - [R3] BLOCKER — §6.4 drift-failure log artifacts are referenced as committed evidence (`regex_drift_failure.log`, `regex_drift_graceful_must.log`, `regex_drift_graceful_may.log`, `regex_drift_lock.json`) but are absent from the provided file set. The manuscript states 'saved' and 'compared' but these files are not in the reproduction package. Either include these files or remove §6.4's claim that they are verifiable committed artifacts.
- [R3] BLOCKER — SQLite URL fragment semantics are undocumented: the lock records `https://www.sqlite.org/pragma.html#pragma_page_size` but `urllib.request.urlopen` silently ignores the fragment and fetches the full `pragma.html`. This must be documented explicitly (e.g., as a note in the lock file and §7.5) so reviewers understand that the SHA256 covers the full page and that fragment-anchored extraction relies on text-search rather than structural navigation.
